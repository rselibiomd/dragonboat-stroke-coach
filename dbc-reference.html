Module 13 Drills

This will provide a few tried and tested drills to help a coach help their paddlers. It is always good practice to keep notes for oneself on what was effective and what can be modified for the next time. Collaborate with the coaches around you. Ask for their ideas and suggestions on how to help paddlers improve their stroke, and/or help the team work as a unit.

Good luck. Helping paddlers start with good technique will be beneficial to everyone.
